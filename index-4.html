About

Practical software for teams that work in the field.

I'm a Flutter/Dart developer at MetaXperts, working on business applications that companies rely on every day: employee attendance, GPS workforce tracking, shop visits, order management and dispatch.

Most of my work sits below the UI — background location that survives OS-level termination, MQTT real-time sync, offline queues that retry over poor connectivity, native Kotlin and Swift modules, and Firebase and Oracle ORDS/APEX integration. I also build with AI: my Jarvis assistant combines Gemini Live, multi-provider fallback, wake-word detection and on-device app automation.

I care about software that keeps working after release — I own Android and iOS build, release and maintenance for the apps I ship.
